# Cosmological Structure Formation and UV Luminosity Functions

**University of Arizona**  
**Advisor:** Prof. Fulvio Melia  
**August 2025–Present**

## Overview

My current research investigates cosmological structure formation and the ultraviolet luminosity function of high-redshift galaxies in different cosmological models, with particular emphasis on the \(R_{\rm h}=ct\) universe.

## Research Questions

This project examines how differences in cosmic expansion history affect:

- The evolution of cosmological perturbations
- The linear matter transfer function
- The matter power spectrum
- The abundance and growth of dark-matter halos
- Star-formation histories
- The ultraviolet luminosity function of high-redshift galaxies

## Numerical Framework

I developed an end-to-end Python pipeline connecting:

1. The linear transfer function
2. The matter power spectrum
3. The halo mass function
4. Halo growth histories
5. Star-formation rates
6. Dust attenuation
7. The predicted galaxy UV luminosity function

The framework enables consistent comparisons between predictions from the standard \(\Lambda\)CDM model and alternative cosmological expansion histories.

## My Contributions

- Developed numerical calculations for linear transfer functions and matter power spectra.
- Reproduced published halo mass functions as validation tests.
- Converted published observational measurements to a common reference cosmology.
- Connected halo growth to star-formation rates and dust-attenuated UV luminosities.
- Quantified the effects of star-formation efficiency, intrinsic scatter, and modeling assumptions.
- Created reproducible and documented Python notebooks for the complete analysis.
